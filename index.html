Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Still stuck? Add it to the notes in the booking form.

How much does a garage floor coating cost?

It depends on the size of the slab, its condition and the system you choose. Send the form with your approximate area and we'll use those details to put together a tailored quote.

Do I need to be home for the estimate?

Usually yes, so the contractor can see the floor in person and check for moisture or damage. They'll confirm the details with you when they get in touch.

Can you coat over an existing epoxy floor?

Sometimes — it depends on how well the old coating is bonded and the condition of the concrete underneath. Mention any peeling or stains in the notes field.
